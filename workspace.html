5. Statistical and analytical tools

Select a method and map the variables. For ELISA, choose the calibration worksheet and the unknown-sample worksheet separately. Map the standard IDs (for example, STD1), concentration and OD columns, then select the assay and map the unknown sample ID and absorbance columns. This supports standards and samples stored on different worksheets. The selected standards are fitted with a 4PL model; if your kit specifies a polynomial, rational or 5PL calibration, this 4PL result will not reproduce that method.

8. Biomedical Analytics

Specialised assay tools are accessed through Analyze → ELISA 4PL standard curve. Calibration standards and unknown samples can be selected from separate worksheets, with a separate curve fitted for each assay.

Further extensions include 5PL fitting, dilution correction, replicate-level QC, assay precision, recovery and parallelism. These require validation against reference datasets before use.

9. Output Viewer

Statistical results and BI summaries appear in their respective sections. Download the statistical analysis report or export the BI summary after running an analysis.
