Editorial and Publishing Policies

These policies are the baseline framework for both Beenorax journals. Each journal's editorial board must approve and publish its final journal-specific procedures before accepting manuscripts for formal peer review.

Editorial independence

Each journal has a separate Editor-in-Chief and editorial board. Editorial decisions must be based on scholarly merit, relevance, methodological quality and ethical compliance, not commercial interests.

Peer review

Manuscripts intended for peer-reviewed publication will undergo editorial screening and independent review. The journal will state the review model and process transparently. Reviewers must declare conflicts of interest and maintain confidentiality.

Research and publication ethics

Authors must confirm originality, disclose funding and conflicts of interest, obtain required ethics approvals and permissions, and accurately report methods and findings. Suspected plagiarism, fabrication, falsification, duplicate submission or authorship disputes will be handled under a documented editorial procedure.

Corrections and retractions

Published records may be corrected, expressions of concern issued, or articles retracted when warranted. Changes will be documented transparently.

Open access and copyright

Each journal must publish its final licence, copyright, repository and any article processing charge policy before submissions open. No publication fee should be represented as applicable until formally approved and disclosed.

Indexing, ISSN and DOI

ISSN, DOI registration, indexing and archiving claims will be made only after confirmation by the relevant registries or services.

Complaints and appeals

Each journal will provide an independent route for complaints and appeals, with conflicts handled by an editor who was not involved in the original decision.
